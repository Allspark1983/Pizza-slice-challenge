The Rush rules

How to play

Your first cut sets the direction. New batches slide in from the left. Swipe through a pizza’s centre, then space your cuts evenly to make its required slices. Finish each batch before its timer runs out. Avoid grenades, even on short swipes.

+10Complete a pizza

−5Wrong complete cut

−10Swipe a grenade

−5Miss a pizza

3 seconds to prepare, then a full 60 seconds. Fresh batches slide in from the left every 5 seconds: 1 pizza at first, 2 after 20 seconds, and 3 after 40 seconds.

  • 10″Small · 4 slices · 2 cuts, 90° apart
  • 12″Medium · 6 slices · 3 cuts, 60° apart
  • 14″Large · 8 slices · 4 cuts, 45° apart

Unfinished pizzas cost 5 points each when the batch expires. Clear it early and wait for the next batch. Your score stays at zero or above. Away from grenades, incomplete swipes and duplicate cuts are ignored.

A win means a new personal best. At the end of the minute, beat the record you started with to win. Ties count as a loss. The leaderboard ranks each player’s best score, then their most pizzas completed in a run.
